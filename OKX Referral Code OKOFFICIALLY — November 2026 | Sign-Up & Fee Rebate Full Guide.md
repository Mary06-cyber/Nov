## 1. What a Fee Rebate Actually Is

A rebate is not "fees automatically discounted by 20%". The mechanism is:

Your standard fee is charged first → eligible trades generate fees → a portion is returned to your account based on the rebate ratio.

So seeing the full fee deducted in your trade history is normal. It does not mean the rebate is missing.

Rebate logic:

- More eligible trades, more rebate
- The rebate ratio is the same for Maker and Taker, calculated on net trading fees
- Buy, sell, and instant convert usually do not count as eligible rebate scenarios
- Spot and perpetual eligible trades are counted
