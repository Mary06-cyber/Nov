## 4. Route B: The Futures Efficiency Track (Fees Are Your Only Certain Edge)

Futures traders count every basis point, so let's do the math properly:

- Futures taker fee 0.060% — **0.048%** after the 20% discount
- With 100,000 USDT monthly volume, the discount alone saves **144 USDT a year**
- Futures maker fee 0.020% — **0.016%** after discount; if you post limit orders, the edge is even bigger

**Three direct recommendations**:

1. **Prefer limit orders over market orders**: the maker rate is roughly a third of the taker rate, and you avoid slippage. Save market orders for moments you truly need instant fills.
2. **Cap your leverage — and write it down**: pick a number (say 5x) and never cross it. Liquidation doesn't come from the market; it comes from "just this once."
3. **Place the stop-loss before opening the position**: entry and stop-loss are one action, not two. Anyone who separates them will eventually hit the "I'll set it in a second" moment.

<strong>The classic mistake on this route</strong>: full-position bets on high leverage. Position sizing exists so that when you're wrong, you're still at the table.

## 5. Route C: The Copy-Trading Risk-Control Track (Picking the Trader Matters Ten Times More Than Copying)

Futures copy trading is Bitget's flagship feature — yet more copy traders lose money than make it, and the difference is made at the "picking" stage:

1. **Look at max drawdown, not just returns**: a trader with 300% returns and an 80% drawdown means you must survive getting halved twice before the profits arrive. Pick small drawdowns and smooth curves.
2. **Match the style to volatility you can stomach**: high-frequency, high-leverage traders post beautiful short-term numbers, but one black swan ends everything. Steady traders' curves look boring — but you can actually hold them.
3. **Keep copy-trading allocation under 20% of total funds**: copy trading is part of a portfolio, not the whole of it. Going all-in on a stranger hands your fate to someone else.
4. **The copy stop-loss ratio is mandatory**: it's the fuse built into the copy system. Do not skip it during setup.

<strong>The classic mistake on this route</strong>: picking traders off the ROI leaderboard. The leaderboard's number one is usually the one with the biggest risk exposure.
