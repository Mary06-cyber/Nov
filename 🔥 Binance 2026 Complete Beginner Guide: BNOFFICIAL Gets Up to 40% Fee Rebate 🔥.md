## 8. Your First Trade: Complete Path

### 8.1 Spot Trading (Practice 1–2 Weeks First)

Navigation: Trade → Spot

Key zones:

- Left: trading pairs (BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT)
- Center: candlestick chart
- Right: order entry

Order types:

| Type | How It Works | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Limit | Executes only at your price or better | Not chasing pumps, set buy-the-dip |
| Market | Instant execution at best price | Urgent entry, small amounts |
| Stop-Limit | Triggers when price hits set level | Stop-loss, breakout entries |

Beginner rules:

- First trade: 5%–10% of total capital only
- Only BTC or ETH
- Use limit orders, avoid market orders chasing green candles
- After buying, don't panic-sell — observe price action for days

### 8.2 Futures & Leverage (Approach Slowly)

Binance futures types:

- **USDT-M Futures**: Margin in USDT, most straightforward
- **Coin-M Futures**: Margin in BTC/ETH, more advanced
- **Options**: Don't touch until you master futures

Default fee (before BNB discount):

- Maker: 0.02%–0.1% (tier-dependent)
- Taker: 0.04%–0.05%

Five terms you must understand:

1. **Cross Margin**: Entire balance backs every position — one bad trade drains all
2. **Isolated Margin**: Only assigned margin at risk
3. **Liquidation Price**: System force-closes when margin falls below maintenance
4. **Funding Rate**: Every 8h, longs pay shorts (or vice versa)
5. **Maintenance Margin**: Minimum collateral to keep position open

Recommended path:

- Practice spot 1–2 weeks
- Start USDT-M with 1–2x leverage, tiny size
- Decide max acceptable loss in dollars before every trade

Never open 50x or 100x on day one. High leverage = fast bankruptcy.

### 8.3 Copy Trading (For Non-Analysts)

- Browse ranked traders by performance
- Select trader(s) to follow
- Your account auto-copies proportional size
- Set total investment cap, per-trade limit, stop-loss

How to pick:

- Max drawdown below 15%
- Track record 3+ months
- Reasonable follower count (extreme numbers = pump-and-dump risk)
- Consistent win rate, not one lucky moonshot

Even with copy trading, set your own total loss limit.
