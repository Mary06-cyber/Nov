## 6. Trading Basics (Spot + Futures + Copy Trading)

### Spot: the only choice for beginners

- "Trade" → "Spot", pick BTC/USDT or ETH/USDT
- **Limit order**: set your price, controllable; **Market order**: instant fill, may suffer slippage
- Beginner rules: majors only, single trade ≤ 5% of total funds, know your stop-loss before buying

### Futures: avoid if you can

- USDT perpetuals offer up to 125x leverage — at 100x, a 1% move liquidates you
- Funding rates settle every 8 hours
- Practice in "Demo Trading" first; never exceed 5x when live

### Copy Trading: Bitget's Signature Feature

Bitget pioneered copy trading — worth a look if you want passive trading:

1. App → "Trade" → "Copy Trading"
2. Review traders' **returns, max drawdown, win rate, follower count**
3. Set your copy amount → confirm

Note: past returns don't guarantee the future, copy trading still needs stop-losses, and don't stake everything on one trader.
