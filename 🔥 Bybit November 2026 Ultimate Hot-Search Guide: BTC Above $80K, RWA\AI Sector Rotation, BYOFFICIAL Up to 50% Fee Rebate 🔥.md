## 8. Your First Trade: Complete Path from Spot to Futures

### 8.1 Spot Trading (Practice for 1–2 Weeks First)

Navigation: Top "Trade" → "Spot"

Key interface zones:

- Left panel: trading pairs (BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT, etc.)
- Center: price chart (candlestick)
- Right panel: order entry box

Order types explained:

| Type | How It Works | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Limit Order | Executes only at your specified price or better | Not chasing pumps, setting buy-the-dip levels |
| Market Order | Executes immediately at best available price | Urgent entry, small amounts, accept slippage |
| Conditional Order | Triggers only when price reaches your set level | Stop-loss placement, breakout entries |

Beginner rules:

- First trade: use only 5%–10% of total deposited capital
- Stick to BTC or ETH only
- Use limit orders, avoid market orders that chase green candles
- After buying, do not panic-sell. Watch price action for several days to build intuition

### 8.2 Futures and Leverage (Approach Slowly)

Bybit futures types:

- **USDT Perpetual**: Margin in USDT, most straightforward for beginners
- **Coin-Margined**: Margin in BTC or ETH, more advanced
- **Options**: Do not touch until you fully understand futures mechanics

Default fee schedule:

- Maker (placing orders on the book): 0.02%
- Taker (taking liquidity): 0.055%

Five terms you must understand before opening any futures position:

1. **Cross Margin**: Entire account balance backs every position — one bad trade can drain everything
2. **Isolated Margin**: Only the margin assigned to that specific position is at risk
3. **Liquidation Price**: When your margin falls below maintenance level, the system force-closes your position
4. **Funding Rate**: Every 8 hours, longs pay shorts (or vice versa) to keep futures price anchored to spot
5. **Maintenance Margin**: Minimum collateral required to keep a position open

Recommended learning path:

- Practice spot trading for 1–2 weeks
- Enable UTA (Unified Trading Account)
- Start futures with 1–2x leverage and tiny position sizes
- Before every trade, decide your maximum acceptable loss in dollars

Never open 50x or 100x leverage on day one. High leverage is not "getting rich fast" — it is "going broke fast."

### 8.3 Copy Trading (For Those Who Do Not Yet Analyze Markets)

Bybit copy trading mechanics:

- Browse ranked traders by performance metrics
- Select one or more traders to follow
- When they open a position, your account automatically copies it at a proportional size
- You can set total investment cap, per-trade limit, and stop-loss parameters

How to pick a trader:

- Maximum drawdown below 15% (not just high total returns)
- Track record of at least 3 months (not a one-week wonder)
- Follower count in reasonable range (extreme follower counts can indicate pump-and-dump dynamics)
- Consistent win rate, not relying on one lucky 100x moonshot

Even with copy trading, set your own total loss limit. Do not blindly follow everything.
