- Face does not match the document

Lv.2 address proof:

- Must show your full name and residential address

## 10. Derivatives-Specific Warnings

- Perpetual contracts have no expiry, price converges through funding rates
- Funding fee equals position value multiplied by the funding rate
- Positive funding: longs pay shorts; negative funding: shorts pay longs
- Funding is collected only at settlement moments while the position is open
- BTCUSDT funding usually settles every 8 hours
- Liquidation uses mark price, not the latest traded price
- Rebate does not protect against leverage loss, slippage, or funding cost
