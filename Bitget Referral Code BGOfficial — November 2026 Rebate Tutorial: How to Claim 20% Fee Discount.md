## 1. What "20% Trading Fee Discount" Actually Means

Many assume 20% means the fee is instantly cut by 20% at order placement. In reality, Bitget's referral rebate is **calculated on your actual net trading fees, then 20% is returned to you** — it does not change the fee rate at the moment you place an order.

| Comparison | Immediate Fee Rate | 20% Referral Rebate |
|---|---|---|
| Deduction method | Full listed rate deducted upfront | Full rate deducted first, then 20% returned later |
| Timing | Instant | Partner commission settles once daily, covering previous day's trades |
| Where shown | Fee column in trade history | Personal Center → My Invitation / Rebate Records |
| User feel | Invisible | Only "felt" when the rebate arrives |

So the fee column in your trade history will still show the **full original fee**. Don't panic — the rebate is settled on a separate track.

Three things to lock in:

1. **The base is "actual net fee paid,"** not trading volume, not profit/loss.

## 8. Three Deposit Routes

**Route A — P2P / C2C (beginner pick)**

- Bitget platform charges no fee; you bear merchant spread, typically 0.5%–1.5%
- Path: Buy Crypto → P2P → Buy USDT
- Filter: verified merchants, high trade count, 98%+ rating, gold badge
- **Payer name must match KYC name**; no third-party payments
- Only click "Paid" after money actually leaves your account
- In-app chat only; no personal QR scans, no external links

Four common P2P scams to memorize:

- Same-amount consecutive orders: merchant opens multiple same-amount orders to confuse you, pays only one but demands all released
- Fake receipt screenshot: clicks "Paid" then sends forged screenshot, money never arrived
- Double-actor release: A really pays but doesn't click paid, B clicks paid using A's screenshot to rush you
- Wrong amount / wrong coin: order 10,000 but only pays 1,000, or pays with wrong coin

**The only truth is the actual arrival status in your own receiving account.** No arrival = no release. Name mismatch = no release.

**Route B — On-chain deposit (already hold crypto)**

- Bitget deposit free; only pay underlying chain gas (to miners, not Bitget)
- Assets → Deposit → select coin → select network
- **Same coin same network** is iron rule: ERC20 / TRC20 / BEP20 / Arbitrum mix-ups can permanently lose funds
- XRP / EOS / TON / XLM / ATOM need Memo / Tag; missing it causes stuck deposits
- First time: test with 1–2 USDT, then transfer large amount after arrival

**Route C — Credit / Debit Card**

- Requires KYC first
- Fee charged by service provider, quote shown on payment page; issuer may decline crypto purchases
- Only for small urgent top-ups, not large amounts
