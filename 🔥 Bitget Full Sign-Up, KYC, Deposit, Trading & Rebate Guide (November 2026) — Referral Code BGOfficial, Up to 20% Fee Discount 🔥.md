### 2. Futures later

Bitget futures are powerful, but beginners often:

- Use 20x–50x leverage immediately
- Don't understand cross/isolated margin

- Average down on losers

Recommended order: spot 1–2 weeks → small futures size → scale gradually.

### 3. Futures terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Cross margin | Whole account backs the position |
| Isolated margin | Only that position's margin at risk |
| Maker | Limit order, provides liquidity, lower fee |
| Taker | Market order, takes liquidity, higher fee |
| Liquidation | Forced close when margin insufficient |
| Funding rate | Periodic payment between longs/shorts |
