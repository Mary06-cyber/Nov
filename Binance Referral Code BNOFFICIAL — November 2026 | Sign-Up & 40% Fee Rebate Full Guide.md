## 5. KYC Identity Verification

Without KYC (Verified), you cannot deposit, trade, or withdraw.

Path: Account Center → Identity Verification → Verify Now

Flow:

1. Select residence and document issuing country
2. Upload front/back of ID (all corners visible, no glare)
3. Face liveness check (blink, turn head)
4. Submit and wait

Practical details:

- Name must match ID exactly, no extra spaces
- Even lighting, remove hat, mask, heavy glasses
- Clear document, no editing, no screen photos
- Review usually within 48 hours, sometimes minutes
- Do not resubmit while pending; it slows things down
