### 2. Futures later, not immediately

OKX Futures is powerful, but beginners often:

- Use 20x–50x leverage too soon
- Don’t understand cross/isolated margin
- Skip stop-loss
- Add to losing positions

Recommended order:

Spot for 1–2 weeks → demo/small size → then futures with strict risk control.

### 3. Futures terms to learn first

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Cross margin | Whole account balance backs the position |
| Isolated margin | Only that position’s margin is at risk |
| Long | Buy low, sell high (bullish) |
| Short | Sell high, buy back lower (bearish) |
| Liquidation | Forced close when margin is insufficient |
| Funding rate | Periodic payment between longs/shorts, not a trading fee |
