## Stop 3: The Futures Zone — The Second Rebate Territory

- Binance futures come in two flavors: USDT-margined (USDT-M) and coin-margined (COIN-M). For most people, USDT-M is all you need.
- Standard fees run about 0.05% Taker and 0.02% Maker; after the 40% rebate, your effective cost is even lower — futures is the most fee-friendly territory on the map.
- Keep leverage at 3–5x as a beginner and position sizes small. Liquidation triggers on the mark price, not the last traded price — during violent wicks, check the mark price before you act.
- One rule to remember: low futures fees save you on fees, but they don't protect your capital — losses in futures compound far faster than in spot. A low fee is not an excuse to crank leverage.

## Stop 4: The Finance Zone (Earn) — Put Idle Money to Work

This is the most overlooked section. Money sitting still has several options in Earn:

- **Flexible savings**: deposit and withdraw anytime. Low yield, high liquidity — good for idle funds.
- **Locked savings**: lock up for a higher yield, redeem at maturity.
- **Dual Investment**: high headline yield, but your funds may be converted into the other asset at the strike price — it's a conditional strategy product. If you don't understand the mechanics, stay away.
- **Launchpad / new-coin mining**: stake per the rules to participate in new project distributions — a signature play of the Binance ecosystem.

One principle: no matter how tempting the yield, never lock all your trading capital into Earn — locked products may penalize early redemption. Keep your liquidity buffer.

## Stop 5: Sub-Accounts and API — Advanced Players' Cloning Technique

- **Sub-accounts**: open accounts under your main account to isolate funds by strategy and risk. Keep strategy money and long-term holdings apart — clean books, no risk contamination.
- **API**: for third-party tools or quant programs. The iron rule is least privilege — grant only what's needed, never enable withdrawal permission unless absolutely required, and set an IP whitelist.

Casual users can browse this section without buying anything, but knowing it exists means you won't panic when your play style levels up.
