## 9. Your First Trade: Complete Path

### 9.1 Spot Trading (Practice 1–2 Weeks First)

Path: Top "Trade" → "Spot"

Key zones:

- Left: trading pairs (BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT)
- Center: candlestick chart
- Right: order entry

| Order Type | How It Works | Best For |
|------------|-------------|----------|
| Limit | Fills only at your price | Not chasing pumps, set buy-the-dip |
| Market | Instant execution at best price | Urgent entry, small amounts |
| Stop-Loss/Take-Profit | Triggers when price hits set level | Stop-loss, breakout entries |

Beginner rules:

- First trade: 5%–10% of total capital only
- Only BTC or ETH
- Use limit orders, avoid market orders chasing green candles
- After buying, don't panic-sell — observe price action for days

### 9.2 Futures & Leverage (Approach Slowly)

Binance futures types:

- **USDT-M Perpetual/Delivery**: USDT margin, most straightforward
- **Coin-M Futures**: BTC/ETH margin, more advanced
- **Options**: Don't touch until you master futures
- **Margin Trading**: Spot borrow-to-amplify, not the same as futures

Default fees (VIP0, check page for actual):

- Spot: maker/taker ~0.10% / 0.10%
- USDT-M Futures: maker ~0.02%, taker ~0.05%

Five terms you must understand:

1. **Cross Margin**: Entire balance backs every position
2. **Isolated Margin**: Only assigned margin at risk
3. **Liquidation Price**: System force-closes when margin falls below maintenance
4. **Funding Rate**: Longs pay shorts (or vice versa) every 8 hours
5. **Maintenance Margin**: Minimum collateral to keep position open

Recommended path:

- Practice spot 1–2 weeks
- Start USDT-M with 1–2x leverage, tiny size
- Decide max acceptable loss in dollars before every trade

Never open 50x or 100x on day one.

### 9.3 Copy Trading & Strategy Bots (For Non-Analysts)

**Copy Trading**:

- Browse ranked traders by performance
- Select trader(s) to follow
- Your account auto-copies proportional size
- Set total investment cap, per-trade limit, stop-loss

How to pick:

- Max drawdown below 15%
- 3+ months track record
- Reasonable follower count (extreme numbers = pump risk)
- Consistent win rate, not one lucky moonshot

**Strategy Bots** (Grid/DCA/Arbitrage):

- Grid trading: profit from ranging markets
- DCA: long-term cost averaging
- Arbitrage: funding rate or cross-exchange spreads

Even with bots, set total loss limit.
