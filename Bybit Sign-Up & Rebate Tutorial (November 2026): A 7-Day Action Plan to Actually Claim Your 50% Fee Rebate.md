## Day 5: First Futures Trade (The Day Beginners Wipe Out)

- Cap leverage at 3–5x and keep the position under 10% of your capital. Futures are for practicing, not gambling.
- **Bybit's "risk limit" system — something other tutorials rarely explain**: the larger your position, the higher the initial and maintenance margin requirements. In other words, at the same 10x leverage, a bigger position pushes the liquidation price closer to you. Keep the risk limit at the lowest tier as a beginner.
- **Liquidation is based on the mark price, not the last traded price.** In violent swings, the last price can wick violently, but as long as the mark price hasn't hit your liquidation price, your position survives. Knowing this saves you from panic-closing on every wick.
- Decide your position mode deliberately: beginners should pick one-way mode — simpler logic, fewer self-inflicted messes.
