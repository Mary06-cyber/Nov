## 6. Trading Basics (Spot + Futures in Brief)

### Spot: the only choice for beginners

- "Trade" → "Spot", pick BTC/USDT or ETH/USDT
- **Limit order**: set your price, controllable; **Market order**: instant fill, but may suffer slippage
- Beginner rules: majors only, single trade ≤ 5% of total funds, know your stop-loss before buying

### Futures: avoid if you can

- USDT perpetuals offer up to 125x leverage — but at 100x, a 1% price move liquidates you
- Funding rates settle every 8 hours; holding through settlement costs/earns funding
- Practice in the "Demo Trading" environment first, and never exceed 5x when you go live

Honestly, for most people: spot + dollar-cost averaging is the optimal strategy.
